# Product

## Register

product

## Users

 Finance directors, finance operators, product reviewers, and implementation stakeholders use this static finance-center demo to inspect platform fund-change posting, affected ledgers, voucher lines, accounting rules, finance statistics, and reports without a backend.

## Product Purpose

This product demonstrates a finance accounting and statistics console based on the provided accounting workbook, account-change fund-impact data, and reference backoffice screenshots. Success means reviewers can understand how each platform fund change generates vouchers, affects ledgers, updates statistics, and appears in finance reports from one local HTML prototype.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, operational.

## Anti-references

Do not make it feel like a marketing landing page, decorative SaaS dashboard, or colorful presentation deck. Avoid oversized hero sections, ornamental cards, playful gradients, and copy that explains obvious controls.

## Design Principles

- Put financial relationships before decoration.
- Keep dense data scannable with stable tables, compact cards, and clear status labels.
- Use Chinese business labels for operator-facing fields.
- Keep business-facing actions read-only or approval-oriented.
- Preserve accounting names and rules unless a module explicitly allows a controlled edit.
- Keep account-change actions, funding impact, vouchers, ledger records, reports, statistics, and MD requirements in sync.
- Keep operational exception handling, external reconciliation execution, and period-close processing outside the foreground module set unless this prototype later owns those production workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, keyboard-friendly native controls, clear focusable actions, reduced-motion-safe interactions, and status colors backed by visible text labels.
